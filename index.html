03 / Jayagoo shopping guide

A useful shortlist needs more than a link.

The most useful Jayagoo spreadsheet finds are the ones you can compare consistently. Keep the same fields for every item, note uncertainties and recheck the listing before payment.

Find your starting point ↗
  1. 01

    Choose one category and one purpose.

    Decide whether you need everyday sneakers, a warm layer, a matching set or a finishing accessory. A focused list is easier to compare.

  2. 02

    Save the exact product and variant.

    Record the listing URL, seller, color, size and date checked. Check which original marketplace links your chosen shopping service accepts; compatibility is not verified here.

  3. 03

    Compare evidence and measurements.

    Review available product photos, written specifications and size charts. Ask for missing information instead of assuming a photo proves quality or authenticity.

  4. 04

    Calculate the full budget.

    Separate the item price from domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping and any applicable taxes or duties. Label estimates clearly.

04 / Straight answers

Before you add it to the list.

Practical answers for readers exploring product spreadsheets, category browsing and shopping comparisons.

What is a Jayagoo spreadsheet?

In this guide, Jayagoo spreadsheet refers to a shopping shortlist organized around product links, categories and comparison notes. This page is an independent discovery guide, not an official Jayagoo service or a downloadable spreadsheet.

How do I use this guide to find products?

Choose a category near the top of the page to open the matching CAFANS category. Compare the listing details and save your preferred product links in your own spreadsheet.

Can I import CAFANS links into Jayagoo?

Compatibility is not verified by this page. Check which original marketplace URLs your chosen service accepts before placing an order.

What should a fashion shopping spreadsheet include?

Include the product URL, seller, category, selected size, measurements, color, listed price, estimated shipping, inspection notes and date checked.

Does a product photo verify brand authenticity?

No. A photograph, brand name or spreadsheet entry does not establish authenticity. Check the seller, provenance, product documentation and return terms separately.

Are prices, availability and delivery times guaranteed?

No. Confirm current stock, variant pricing, service charges, shipping options and applicable import costs with the relevant seller or service before payment.
